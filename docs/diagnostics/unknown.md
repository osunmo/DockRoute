# UNKNOWN

**Kind:** Abstention.

Available evidence cannot support a reliable more specific conclusion.

## Example

A dependency has no known port, a destination is ambiguous, or probing is unavailable during recreation.

## Suggested fix

Review the intention and observations; supply an explicit check or collect relevant evidence where appropriate.

## What it does not establish

It is neither evidence of health nor a network failure. It never counts as a successful root-cause diagnosis.
